# 🛒 Urban Mart — Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

A professional **Power BI retail analytics project** designed to transform sales data into actionable business insights.

## 📊 Project Overview

Urban Mart is a retail business analytics project focused on understanding sales performance, profitability, customers, products, discounts, and regional performance.

The dashboard was designed to help business stakeholders quickly identify trends, high-performing areas, customer behavior, and opportunities for improving sales and profitability.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Monitor overall sales and profit performance
* Analyze monthly sales and profit trends
* Identify high-performing products and categories
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Compare regional and segment performance
* Analyze discount impact on sales
* Provide interactive business insights for decision-making

## 📁 Dashboard Pages

The Urban Mart Power BI report includes multiple analytical pages covering:

1. **Executive Overview**
2. **Sales & Product Analysis**
3. **Customer Analysis**
4. **Sales & Profit Trends**
5. **Discount & Scenario Analysis**
6. **Management Dashboard**
7. **Customer Detail Analysis**

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

### Sales Performance

Analysis of total sales, growth, monthly performance, and overall business trends.

### Customer Analysis

Understanding customer contribution, purchasing patterns, and customer-level performance.

### Product Analysis

Identification of top-performing products, categories, and areas requiring attention.

### Profitability

Evaluation of profit performance and relationships between sales, discounts, and profitability.

### Discount Scenario Analysis

Interactive scenario analysis to understand how changes in discount levels can affect sales outcomes.

This dashboard converts raw retail data into an interactive decision-support tool.

It enables stakeholders to move from **"What happened?"** to **"Why did it happen?"** and explore potential business scenarios.
